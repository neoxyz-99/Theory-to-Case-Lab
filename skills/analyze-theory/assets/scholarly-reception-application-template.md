### Debate Map (only when it adds value)

Use a Mermaid debate map only when three or more positions are difficult to compare linearly. Before the diagram, explain its purpose, encoding, evidence class, and inferential limit. Link every position to a verified source in the table or prose, then add `**Interpretation:**`. Do not exceed two diagrams unless the user asks.

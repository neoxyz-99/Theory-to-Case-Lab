## Confidence guide

- `High`: checked directly in an accessible source with a precise locator.
- `Medium`: source verified but evidence or locator is incomplete.
- `Low`: discovery lead, partial access, OCR uncertainty, or inference requiring review.

## Audit questions

- Does the cited passage support the exact scope and modality of the claim?
- Is a primary source available for a claim currently supported secondarily?
- Does the row contain interpretation that should be separated as `[A]`?
- Is contrary or limiting evidence represented?
- Does the intended paper section use the claim for the same purpose as the source?
